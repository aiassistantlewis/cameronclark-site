How offers work

Three steps at the counter

  1. Sort

    We go through your cards with you and pull anything with real value. Commons are bought by the box.

  2. Price

    Each card is checked against recent sold prices, not asking prices, and you see every comp on screen.

  3. Get paid

    Take cash on the spot, or more in store credit if you'd rather trade into something from the case.
