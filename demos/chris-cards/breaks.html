How it works

Three ways to break

Pick your team

Buy the team you collect. Every card for that team in the case is yours. Popular teams cost more, smaller markets cost less.

Random teams

Every spot is the same price. Teams are drawn live on camera once the break fills, so anyone can land the big one.

Hit draft

All the autos and numbered cards go in one pile, and buyers draft in order. Good for collectors who want hits, not base.

Break questions

Do I have to be there in person?

No. Claim a spot by phone or in store, then watch the stream. Your cards are held at the counter for 30 days or shipped for a flat $5.

What if my team gets nothing?

It happens in any break. That's why the team prices are posted before every break, so you know the odds you're buying.

Are breaks for kids?

Kids are welcome with a parent. The Kids Card Club runs separately, with a free pack and no buying needed.
