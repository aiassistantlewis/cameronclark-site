Before you visit

Do you take cards on trade?

Yes. Trades are valued like store credit, so you get more toward a card in the case than you would in cash.

How do I pay?

Cash, all major cards and tap to pay. Store credit never expires.

Can you hold a card for me?

Call the shop and we'll hold any card in the case for 48 hours.
