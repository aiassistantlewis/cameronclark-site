Große Gruppen

Ten or more? We love a big table.

For parties over 12, call us or send the form above with a note and we'll hold a long table. Groups of 15 or more choose a set menu so the kitchen can keep up, and an 18% service charge is added.

  • 10 to 16

    Stammtisch table

    A full communal table under the roof, ideal for birthdays and teams.

    Bierstube buyout

    The whole indoor hall. Food and drink minimum from $2,500.
