How it works

From first email to last petal

  1. InquirySend your date and venue. We confirm availability within two business days.
  2. ConsultationA 45-minute call or visit to the shop to talk colors, style and budget.
  3. ProposalA mood board, flower list and itemized quote. A 30% deposit holds your date.
  4. Final detailsSix weeks out we lock counts, timeline and any last changes.
  5. Wedding dayWe deliver, set up and pin every boutonniere before photos.
