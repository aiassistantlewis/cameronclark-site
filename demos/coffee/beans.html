Brew it like we do

Our home ratios

Use 1 gram of coffee for every 16 grams of water, water just off the boil, and grind right before you brew.

  • Pour-over22 g coffee · 350 g water · 3:30
  • French press30 g coffee · 480 g water · 4:00
  • Espresso18 g in · 38 g out · 28 sec
  • Cold brew100 g coffee · 1 L water · 18 hrs
