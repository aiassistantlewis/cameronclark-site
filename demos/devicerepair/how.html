FAQ

Questions we hear at the counter

Still wondering about something? Call us at (727) 555-0185 and a technician will pick up.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome every day we're open. Booking ahead holds a bench slot and the part for your model, so it's the fastest way in on Saturdays.

Will I lose my photos or apps?

Screen, battery, port and back glass repairs don't touch your storage. We still recommend an iCloud or Google backup before any repair, and we'll tell you up front if a job could affect data.

Do you need my passcode?

Usually not. Some tests, like Face ID or a full touch check, are faster with it. If you'd rather not share it, we test at the counter with you instead.

What parts do you use?

Premium OLED and LCD screens and new high-capacity batteries, with OEM-grade parts available on most iPhone and Galaxy models. We show you the part grade and price before we start.

What does the warranty cover?

One year on the part and our labor: dead pixels, touch issues, battery health drops and port failures. It doesn't cover new drops, cracks or liquid damage.

Will a repair void my manufacturer warranty?

If your device is still under the maker's warranty, we'll tell you and point you there first. For out-of-warranty devices, independent repair is legal and common.

How do I pay?

At pickup, once you've checked the repair. We take cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash. Nothing is charged at drop-off.
