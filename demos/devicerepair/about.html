Data privacy promise

Your phone is your life. We treat it that way.

Every ticket follows the same written policy, and every technician signs it. If you want it in writing, we'll print it on your receipt.

  1. We don't open your apps. Repairs are tested with built-in diagnostics, not by browsing your photos or messages.
  2. Passcodes are optional. If a test needs one, it's stored on the ticket only until pickup, then deleted.
  3. Benches are on camera. Footage is kept 30 days and covers every device from check-in to pickup.
  4. Wipes come with a certificate. Ask us to erase a device and you get a dated wipe report.
