Why Fixwell

Three promises printed on every ticket.

  • 1-year warranty

    Parts and labor on every repair. If the screen ghosts or the battery fades, bring it back and we redo it free.

  • No fix, no fee

    Diagnosis is free. If we can't repair it, you don't pay a cent, and you get a written note on what we found.

  • Your data stays private

    Most repairs never need your passcode. When a test does, we use it at the bench in front of a camera and delete it from the ticket at pickup.
