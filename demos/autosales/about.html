About Sunpoint

A family lot that sells cars the way we would want to buy one

Ray and Marisol Delgado opened Sunpoint with eleven cars and a folding table in 1998. Their daughter Tess runs it now, with the same rules.

Our story

Three rules since 1998.

Ray sold cars at a big-box dealer for ten years and hated the four-square worksheet. When he opened his own lot on 34th Street, he made three rules: post the real price, show the car's history, and take it back if it's wrong.

Twenty-eight years and more than 9,000 cars later, those rules are still taped to the office window. Tess added a fourth in 2019: answer every review, good or bad, within a day.

How pricing works

One price. Posted. Explained.

1

Priced to market on day one

Before a car hits the lot we compare it with similar cars within 150 miles and price it in the lowest fifth. That's why we don't negotiate.

2

No add-ons hiding in the paperwork

No nitrogen tires, no etching, no surprise packages. Extended warranties are optional and quoted separately.

3

One fee, on every sticker

Florida sales tax, tag and title, plus a $499 dealer fee for paperwork and registration. That's the whole list.
