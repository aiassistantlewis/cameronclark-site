The room

A 1920s storefront, lit like it's still Prohibition.

Behind an unmarked green door on 2nd Street North there's a terrazzo floor, a long walnut bar and more candles than the fire marshal strictly loves. The music stays low enough to hear the person next to you.

Our bartenders work from a short, seasonal list and a long memory for classics. Tell them what you usually drink and they'll get you somewhere new.
