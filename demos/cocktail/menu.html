Behind the bar

The small things nobody sees.

  • Clear ice, cut by hand

    We freeze 300-pound blocks and cut them down every afternoon, so your Old Fashioned melts slowly and looks like glass.

  • Juice pressed twice a day

    Citrus goes flat in hours. We press at 4 PM and again at 9 PM.

  • Syrups and tinctures made here

    Roasted pineapple, wildflower honey, sea salt, smoked black tea. Nothing comes out of a plastic bottle.
