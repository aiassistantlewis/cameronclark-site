Before you come

Good to know

21 and over

Everyone needs a valid photo ID at the door, every night, including private events.

Dress code

Smart casual. Collared shirts are welcome, tank tops and athletic wear are not. Hats off at the bar, please.

Parking

Metered street parking nearby, plus a public garage a short walk away.

Walk-ins

All 14 bar seats are kept for walk-ins. Weeknights are easy. Fridays after 9 PM, expect a short wait.

Accessibility

Step-free entry and an accessible restroom. Ask for a table near the door.

Getting home

We'll happily call you a ride. Rideshare pickups are easiest right out front.
