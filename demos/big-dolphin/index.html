House Cleaning Across Tampa Bay

Big Dolphin Cleaning is a locally owned cleaning company serving homes and businesses across Pinellas, Hillsborough and Pasco counties. Whether you need a standard house cleaning to keep things on track, a top-to-bottom deep clean after a busy season, or a move-in or move-out clean before the keys change hands, we bring the supplies and the checklist.

Homes here take a beating. Humidity feeds mildew in shower grout, sand finds its way into every rug, and spring pollen coats the lanai. That is why our visits put extra attention on bathrooms, floors and the outdoor living spaces Florida homes rely on. Hosts on the beaches lean on our vacation rental turnovers, and busy families save with recurring cleaning plans.

We clean in St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Largo, Tampa, Brandon, Wesley Chapel and the communities in between. See every area we serve.

A Note From the Owners

We started Big Dolphin because we grew up around the water here and know how much a Tampa Bay home goes through: sand tracked in from the beach, humid summers that never quite let the bathroom dry, and weekends too good to spend scrubbing.

FAQ

Cleaning Questions, Answered

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients leave a key, a lockbox or a door code. We will confirm entry details by text before the visit and lock up when we leave.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. Your team brings vacuums, mops, cloths and cleaning products. If you prefer a specific product or have allergies, tell us and we will use yours.

How is my price set?

By the size of your home, the type of clean and how often you book. You see the price before you confirm. [Confirm pricing method]
