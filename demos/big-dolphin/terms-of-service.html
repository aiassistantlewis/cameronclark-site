Terms of Service

Last updated November 18, 2026

These terms apply when you book a cleaning with Big Dolphin Cleaning Co. ("Big Dolphin", "we"). By booking, you agree to them.

Quotes and prices

Prices on this website are starting prices. Your final price depends on the size and condition of your home and the service you choose, and we confirm it with you before your visit. If your home needs much more work than described, we will check with you before going over the agreed price.

Access to your home

Please make sure we can get in at the scheduled time, whether you are home or leave a key, lockbox or door code. If we cannot get in and you did not let us know at least 24 hours ahead, the visit may be charged as a late cancellation.

Cancellations and changes

You can cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours notice at no charge. Changes with less notice may carry a late-change fee, and we will always tell you the amount first.

Recurring plans

Recurring discounts apply after your first visit while your plan is active. There is no long-term contract, and you can pause, change or cancel your plan at any time.

The re-clean promise

If something on our checklist was missed, tell us within 48 hours of your visit and we will come back to re-clean those items at no charge.

Payment

Payment is due on the day of service. We will tell you the accepted payment methods when you book.

Safety and limits

For safety, our teams do not move heavy furniture, climb above a two-step ladder, clean biohazards, or clean up after pests or mold growth. Please put away valuables, cash and fragile keepsakes before your visit.

Pets

Please tell us about your pets before the visit. For everyone’s safety, we may ask that a pet stay in a separate room or crate.

Damage

We take care with your home. If something is damaged during a visit, tell us within 24 hours, with photos if you can, and we will review it with you and work toward a fair fix. We are not responsible for items that were already damaged, worn or improperly installed.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. The current version is always on this page.

Contact us

Questions about these terms? Call or text (603) 760-8134, Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 6 PM.
