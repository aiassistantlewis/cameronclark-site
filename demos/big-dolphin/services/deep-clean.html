A deep clean goes past the surfaces. We hand-wipe baseboards, scrub grout where Florida humidity leaves mildew, clean inside the microwave and get the fan blades, vents and door frames a quick clean leaves behind.

FAQ

Deep Cleaning Questions

How often should I get a deep clean?

Most homes benefit from a deep clean [two to four times a year], with standard visits in between.

Do you move furniture?

We clean under and behind light furniture we can move safely. We do not move heavy pieces.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients leave a key, a lockbox or a door code. We will confirm entry details by text before the visit and lock up when we leave.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. Your team brings vacuums, mops, cloths and cleaning products. If you prefer a specific product or have allergies, tell us and we will use yours.

How is my price set?
