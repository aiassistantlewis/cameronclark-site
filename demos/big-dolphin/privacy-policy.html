Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Big Dolphin Cleaning Co. ("Big Dolphin", "we") collects when you use this website or book a cleaning, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or book, we collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, and the details you share about your home, such as size, pets and entry instructions. Like most websites, our hosting provider may also record basic technical data such as your browser type and the pages you visit.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses Google Tag Manager and related analytics tools, which may set cookies, to count visits and see which pages and buttons people use. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How we use it

We use your information to send quotes, schedule and complete your cleaning, send reminders and on-the-way texts, take payment, answer your questions and improve our service.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, you agree that we may call or text you about your quote, booking and visits. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. We do not send marketing texts without your separate permission.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the people and tools that help us run the business, such as our cleaners for your visit, and our scheduling, payment and messaging providers, and only as much as they need to do that work. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Entry details

Door codes, lockbox codes and key instructions are used only for your visits, shared only with the team assigned to your home, and deleted when you end service.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a client and for a reasonable time afterward for our records, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time.

Children

This website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date.

Contact us

Questions about your information? Call or text (603) 760-8134, Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 6 PM.
