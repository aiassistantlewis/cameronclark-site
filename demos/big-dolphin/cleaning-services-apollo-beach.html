FAQ

Cleaning Questions, Answered

Do you clean homes in all of Apollo Beach?

We cover most of Apollo Beach. Send your ZIP code with a quote request and we will confirm.

How soon can you clean in Apollo Beach?

Often within a few days. Call or text to check open dates.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients leave a key, a lockbox or a door code. We will confirm entry details by text before the visit and lock up when we leave.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. Your team brings vacuums, mops, cloths and cleaning products. If you prefer a specific product or have allergies, tell us and we will use yours.

How is my price set?

By the size of your home, the type of clean and how often you book. Starting prices are on our services page, and you see your exact price before you confirm.

Who will be cleaning my home?

A Big Dolphin team trained on our written checklist. Recurring clients keep the same team whenever we can.
