Our story

Our Story

Big Dolphin Cleaning Co. started with a simple idea: people in Tampa Bay should be able to book a great cleaning as easily as they book a fishing charter, and trust the people who show up.

Two partners started Big Dolphin in St. Petersburg in 2026. We wanted a cleaning company that answers texts quickly, shows up when it says it will and fixes things fast when something is missed.

We clean homes, vacation rentals and small businesses from St. Pete to Wesley Chapel, with the same checklist and the same promise on every visit.

A Note From the Owners

We started Big Dolphin in St. Petersburg in 2026. We live here, and we know how much a Tampa Bay home goes through: sand tracked in from the beach, humid summers that never quite let the bathroom dry, and weekends too good to spend scrubbing.

Our goal is simple. Show up when we say we will, clean like it is our own place, and fix it fast if we ever miss something. If you ever have a question, you get a real person, not a call center.

Cam & Robert

Owners, Big Dolphin Cleaning Co.
