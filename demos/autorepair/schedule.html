Before you come in

How long will my car be here?

Oil changes, batteries and tires are usually done while you wait. Most other repairs are ready the same day if you drop off by 9 AM.

Where does the shuttle go?

Anywhere within 5 miles, including downtown, the Grand Central District and Gulfport. It runs from 7:30 AM to 5 PM on weekdays.

Do I pay for the inspection?

The digital inspection is free with any service. Check-engine diagnostics are $99, credited toward the repair if you have us fix it.

Can I bring my own parts?

Yes, for most jobs. We charge labor only, and the warranty covers our labor but not the part itself.
