How selling works

Four steps, about twenty minutes

  1. 1

    Book a buying slot

    Pick a 20-minute slot with the form below. Bring up to 30 pieces per visit so every item gets a real look, not a glance.

  2. 2

    Bring clean items on hangers

    Freshly washed and on hangers, please, not in a trash bag. We see fit and length faster, and your pieces don't arrive wrinkled.

  3. 3

    Sort it together

    A buyer goes through each piece in front of you and says why it's a yes or a pass. Offers usually land at 30 to 40% of our shelf price.

  4. 4

    Get paid on the spot

    Cash in hand, or 50% more in store credit. Passes go home with you, or into our monthly donation run to a St. Pete shelter.

Buying now: suede, flannel, chunky knits and ’90s denim

What we buy, what we pass on

Yes, please

  • Denim made before about 2005, any wash, any rips
  • Band, tour, sports and souvenir tees, single-stitch especially
  • Leather and suede: jackets, boots, belts and bags
  • ’60s to ’90s dresses and skirts with original labels
  • Workwear: chore coats, carpenter pants, coveralls
  • Y2K: baby tees, slip dresses, track jackets, platforms
  • Western shirts, cowboy boots and pearl snaps

We'll pass

  • Fast fashion from the last ten years
  • Anything stained, musty or covered in pet hair
  • Pilled knits and stretched-out elastic
  • Formal gowns and wedding dresses
  • Underwear, socks and swimwear
  • Shoes with worn-through soles or cracked leather
  • Newer luxury pieces, which need a consignment shop

Before you come in

Good questions

Not sure your pile is a fit? Text three photos to (603) 760-8134 and a buyer will reply the same day.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

For 5 pieces or fewer, yes, Tuesday to Thursday if a buyer is free. Anything bigger needs a slot so you're not waiting around.

How do you decide what to pay?

We price each piece for our floor first, then offer 30 to 40% of that in cash. Rare denim, leather and band tees can go higher. We always tell you the number per item.

Do I need to bring anything?

A photo ID. We record every purchase, and we can't buy from anyone under 18.

Do you consign?

No. We buy outright so you're paid today and never have to check back on a sale.
